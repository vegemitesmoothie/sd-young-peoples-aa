Young & Sober
in San Diego

If AA felt boring, you were probably in the wrong room. San Diego has a whole young people’s AA scene — meetings full of people in their 20s, sober dances, campouts, and a conference circuit. AA defines “young people” as under 30, but as GSDYPAA puts it, it’s for the young and the young at heart. Same program, much better company.

This page was made for you — by someone who cares about you and wants you around. No sign-up, no lecture. Just the rooms where the young sober people are, and the stuff that makes it actually fun.

Something to read

Free · from AA itself

Living SoberAA’s little book of practical tips

Living Sober

First printed 1975 · General Service Conference-approved literature

Short, practical, and funny in places — hour-by-hour methods for not drinking: the 24-hour plan, what to do at a drinking party, how to handle loneliness, anger, and “should I keep liquor in the house?” It’s the book people hand to newcomers for a reason. AA posts it free on aa.org, split into sections:

  • Pages 1–17 — how to use the booklet, staying away from the first drink, the 24-hour plan
  • Pages 33–49 — fending off loneliness, anger and resentments, being good to yourself
  • Pages 63–71 — being wary of drinking occasions, letting go of old ideas
  • Pages 89–92 — appendix & the Twelve Steps

Why links, not a download: it’s AA’s copyrighted book, and they give it away free on their own site — so this page points you to their official copies. AA also posts a free audio version on aa.org.
